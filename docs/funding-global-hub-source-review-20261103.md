The fellowship page also says **closed** as of 11 November; its own deadline must not be confused with the Global Economic Prosperity cutoff. - One source-bound list: eleven publisher references in three geographic groups, including **all 54 African project-location choices** and Outside Africa (location is *not* citizenship). Filter by scope, category and local text; no automatic HTTP fetches.
- Every card displays official link, geographic warning, type, original publisher review date, unverified current application window and non-eligibility preparation status. Preserve existing per-programme age, research-funding and registration constraints.
- Preserve `CAPITAL_SOURCE_RECHECK_ON = '2026-12-02'` as the conservative **last review-valid South African calendar day for the original 2 November sources**, not a global expiry applied to newly checked publishers. Under the per-publisher 30-day policy, 2 November sources become overdue at 00:00 SAST on **3 December**, 3 November sources at 00:00 SAST on **4 December**, and the independently reviewed 11 November UNDP/GEF sources at 00:00 SAST on **12 December 2026**. These dates are **review recheck deadlines**, not open-call evidence, financial eligibility or new source verification. Invalid/impossible or future publisher review stamps must fail closed; do not reset old records from another publisher's review. This policy difference requires editorial approval before merge. When called with `OTHER`, Africa-only records must not be presented as aligned, but international programmes remain discoverable subject to original country/impact restrictions.
