# LUQI / KHORO — One Africa + International Funding Hub
**Source check:** 3 November 2026 SAST. **Location:** `/#/african-radar` via existing `/#/opportunities` funding CTA. KHORO is integrated into Luqi under the existing login.
**Scope:** Existing offline, no-AI-credit programme-references/worksheet workflow, *not* live grant availability, eligibility clearance, VC introductions or funded applications. No data leaves the browser and no login is added.

## Original publisher programme evidence
The prior South African records **retain their last substantive review date of 2 November 2026** and existing original sources: [TIA](https://www.tia.org.za/funding-instruments/), [IDC](https://www.idc.co.za/faq/) and [NYDA](https://www.nyda.gov.za/Products-Services/NYDA-Grant-Programme.html). Their eligibility exclusions remain in force for applicants outside South Africa.

New records, **substantively checked 3 November 2026**:
1. **All-African entrepreneurship:** [Tony Elumelu Foundation, official TEF Entrepreneurship Programme](https://www.tonyelumelufoundation.org/tef-entrepreneurship-programme), especially *Application Phase* and *Applicant Eligibility Criteria*. The programme describes entrepreneurs from all 54 African countries, 18+ and businesses/ideas up to five years old for the 2026 round. Its [older official FAQ](https://www.tonyelumelufoundation.org/tef-entrepreneurship-programme/faqs) has inconsistent business-age figures (three versus five), so **verify the selected cohort**. Applications in the advertised 2026 round ran January–March; no new open window is asserted.
2. **Sub-Saharan business challenge finance:** [AECF competitions — approach and funding process](https://www.aecfafrica.org/approach/competitions/), see *Using challenge funds* and *Our funding process*; see [specific opportunities](https://www.aecfafrica.org/im-looking-to/see-aecfs-funding-opportunities/). The exact country/sector and matching-funding conditions depend on each competition; a Pan-African discovery tag is **not universal eligibility**. The publisher states it does not charge applicants.
3. **Women-led SME access through financial partners:** [AfDB AFAWA — Access to Finance](https://afawa.afdb.org/pillars/access-to-finance), *Access to Finance* section. Lines of credit / guarantees are delivered through financial institutions, **not direct AfDB cash grants**. Applicants should confirm actual bank, country coverage, interest rates and terms; Luqi collects no gender or financial records.
4. **International poverty-impact innovation:** [Global Innovation Fund — What we fund](https://www.globalinnovation.fund/what-we-fund) and [FAQ](https://www.globalinnovation.fund/frequently-asked-questions), *Who can apply / Where does GIF invest*. Organisations can be located worldwide, but the innovation must serve people living in poverty in developing countries. Individual innovators are encouraged to apply via an affiliated organisation. No open award or grant amount is asserted.
5. **Global innovation challenges:** [MIT Solve — Challenges](https://solve.mit.edu/challenges), *Open and Upcoming Challenges*, and individual [2027 Global Economic Prosperity Challenge](https://solve.mit.edu/challenges/2027-global-economic-prosperity-challenge?tab=overview). Global challenge approach; some published 2027 challenge deadlines are **6 November 2026 at noon US Eastern time**. This catalogue does not expose a fabricated perpetual "open" state; applicants must verify the exact challenge, eligibility, deadline and time zone at submission.
