# Luqi-AI: Africa-first learning and integrations roadmap

## Product requirements
- Unlimited *learning pathways*, not unlimited paid inference: progress from foundational literacy through advanced professional training without artificial subject or skill ceilings.
- Universal standards: truthful answers, privacy, accessible design, respectful treatment and African-language quality testing on all plans.
- Cost-aware access: community, sponsored, student, everyday, professional and enterprise offerings; higher tiers purchase compute capacity and service levels, not truthfulness.
- Practice-based learning: teach → simulate → assess → remediate → portfolio → opportunity. Educators can author and review curricula.
- Offline-first: cache licensed learning materials and deterministic browser-based simulations; clearly distinguish offline functionality from online AI features.
- 3D/4D learning: modular physics, bioscience, electrical, agricultural, trades and engineering simulations, with device capability fallback and validated mathematical models.
- Independent validation: do not claim superiority, zero downtime, immutable archives, curriculum compliance or broad native-language coverage without reproducible evidence.

## Integration interface (proposed, not activated)
Provider adapters must expose supported countries, languages, capabilities, price schedule, service health, rate limits, security and data-processing terms. No automatic third-party access to companion memories.

Candidate families:
- African language technology: Lelapa AI/Vulavula (contract and pricing review), Masakhane research resources (per-asset licensing review).
- Payments: Paystack and Flutterwave (country-by-country method availability, sandbox first, idempotency and webhook verification).
- Curriculum and opportunities: approved local publishers, educators, accredited bodies and verified opportunities (provenance and refresh dates required).

## Required engineering gates
1. Repair existing CI type-check failures and run all tests.
2. Add an atomic persistent usage ledger with reservations, reconciliation, unique request IDs and hard server-side caps; do not rely on the stateless budget helpers alone.
3. Connect trusted provider usage to metering, verify unit prices and FX rates; display estimates separately from final charges.
4. Introduce feature flags for every paid provider and pilot rollout; keep production disabled until approved.
5. Establish dataset provenance and content-license review. Noncommercial data must never silently enter a commercial product.
6. Validate simulations against known reference calculations and test mobile, offline, low-bandwidth and accessibility behavior.
7. Replace promotional comparison claims with measured benchmarks, date-stamped methodology and reproducible results.

## Definition of done
No integration is considered operational until credentials, privacy review, provider terms, error handling, observability, cost ceiling, automated tests and rollback have been verified in staging.
