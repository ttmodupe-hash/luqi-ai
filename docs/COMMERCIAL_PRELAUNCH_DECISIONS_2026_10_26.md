# LUQI-AI — commercial prelaunch decisions (not an offer for sale)

**Prepared:** 26 October 2026 (SAST). **Status:** no merchant approval, no live checkout, no active paid membership, no customer orders or subscription billing. This document prepares the business decisions only.

## First principle: sell what can be delivered, not what is on the roadmap

| Stage | Customer receives | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free / limited pilot | Existing browser physics, local calculations, business/study drafting, local silent story studio, and account-owned notes where accepted; separate limited AI text trial subject to sign-in, provider consent and quota | Existing pilot; **not broad-launch approval** |
| Proposed one-time assisted Creator Pack | Human-assisted six-scene **90-second silent** animation/storyboard, script and subtitle deliverables; customer reviews and uploads themselves | **Not on sale**. Delivery capacity, seller, price, refund and merchant approval missing |
| Proposed affordable membership | A recurring, clearly metered and supported bundle of proven features, with a stated AI allowance only after actual cost/quality evidence and server-side entitlement enforcement | **Not available**. No automatic subscriber charges or 'unlimited' promise |

The first possible paid experiment is a **human-assisted one-time service**, not an automatic generative-video or subscription product. This may avoid promising expensive ongoing model usage before cost evidence exists. It still requires real delivery capacity, customer terms and merchant readiness.

## Unit-economics worksheet — owner fills privately

Before choosing a price or subscription tier, record the following for each proposed product in a controlled business record:

| Variable | Evidence required | Current value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Currency, seller and jurisdiction | Verified merchant identity and settlement account | **Unverified** |
| Price, tax presentation and payment fees | Approved checkout quote and local tax advice where needed | **Not approved** |
| Human work per order or member | Timed real delivery/review/support pilot | **Unmeasured** |
| Provider inference cost | Actual Gemini/OpenAI bills for the correct project and period | **Unreconciled** |
| Storage/hosting/support allocation | Actual invoices and consented usage | **Unmeasured** |
| Refund/dispute provision | Approved policy and processor settlement behaviour | **Unverified** |
| Capacity and fulfillment time | Demonstrated completed work on real supported devices | **Unverified** |
| Target contribution margin | Owner-approved commercial policy | **Not set** |

For a one-time order, **contribution = amount retained after applicable tax and payment fees − direct labor − actual model/storage/delivery costs − refund provision**. For a recurring plan, compute this **per active subscriber per billing period**, then subtract fixed costs to evaluate business viability. Do not present a profitable price without measured inputs; do not treat gross revenue as cash available to the founder.

### AI cost boundary that must remain intact

- Approved API allocation: **USD 100 per month**, Gemini **USD 60**, OpenAI **USD 40**; preserve provider-side limits and **auto-reload OFF**.
- The existing authenticated text pilot has a daily account attempt limit and a database reservation mechanism. **A USD 0.10 attempt reservation is not an actual provider bill**, and the pilot limit is not a paid membership entitlement.
- Gemini and OpenAI actual charges must be reviewed for the correct projects, with period, currency and scope verified. Unavailable invoices are **unverified**, not zero.
- No unlimited inference, unlimited video/voice or guaranteed 24/7 availability at any proposed price. A $100 shared budget cannot be used as proof of profitable subscriptions.
- Keep new paid model calls, provider subscriptions, advertising purchases and recurring commitments OFF until an authorised operator explicitly approves them.

## Merchant/checkout acceptance — human-controlled

1. Confirm seller legal identity, country, banking/settlement currency, processor eligibility and tax responsibilities. Do not assume a provider supports the seller's country.
2. Approve the exact product scope, price, turnaround, revision allowance, asset rights, cancellation/refund policy, support contact and data retention.
3. Use a **merchant-owned hosted checkout** only after onboarding and approval. A test payment, redirect, payment link or screenshot is not proof of settlement.
4. Verify an actual permitted transaction and its receipt/settlement/refund/dispute lifecycle with the owner. Never paste merchant exports, bank details or customer identifiers into GitHub or chat.
5. For future in-app subscriptions, separately review signed webhooks, replay resistance, order/owner binding, durable server-side entitlement state, revocation/refunds, quotas and real MySQL isolation. The disconnected [PR #264](https://github.com/ttmodupe-hash/luqi-ai/pull/264) is a candidate **evidence ledger**, not a connected merchant or approved paid product.
6. Keep legacy wallet/checkout routes closed. Do not turn on customer payments just to see whether checkout works.

## A low-cash launch sequence

1. Improve the existing self-service pilot and collect permissioned feedback **only after** the support/privacy/recovery gates.
2. Identify the most valued working feature from real observed outcomes, not fictional signups or conversion forecasts.
3. Time one owner-approved assisted Creator Pack rehearsal **without charging**, with a licensed, non-sensitive test brief; confirm output quality and actual labor.
4. Obtain merchant/seller and refund acceptance; choose a price from measured costs and customer willingness to pay.
5. With explicit approval, pilot a small number of paid orders **only after** checkout, receipts, refunds, support and fulfillment are operationally verified.
6. Design recurring subscriptions only when genuine repeat value, provider cost, durable entitlements, quotas and support capacity are proven.
