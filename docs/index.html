Capabilities

Six modes, one rule: an answer you can check

Every capability returns sources, citations or an honest “unverified” label — never a confident guess dressed up as fact.

Scam Shield

Paste the message or describe the scheme. Get a risk score, the matched fraud patterns, the questions to ask, and who to report to.

Try: “Invest R500, get R5000 back in 7 days, guaranteed” → critical risk, 4 patterns matched.

Everyday Services

Plain-language guides to the SRD grant, SASSA pensions, SARS filing season, UIF claims and NSFAS applications — steps, official channels, scam warnings.

Try: “How do I appeal a declined SRD grant?” → the ITSAA process, deadlines and contacts.

African History Archive

A curated, sourced archive from Kush to 1994 — civilizations, leaders, resistance and liberation. Debates are labelled as debates, not settled fiction.

Try: “Great Zimbabwe” → the stone kingdom, its gold trade, and three verifiable sources.

Deep Research

Multi-source investigation with citations you can resolve — or an honest UNVERIFIED label and a guided path to try again. No dead ends.

Try: “What does research say about moringa?” → cited papers from scholarly indexes.

The Professor

An adaptive tutor that follows your level, quizzes you, and tracks your progress — any subject, at your pace, in your language.

At launch: requires a free LUQI account to track your progress.

Money Skills

Budgeting, saving, debt, TFSAs and investing basics — clear guidance with real numbers and a disclaimer on every answer. Education, not advice.

Try: “Explain compound interest simply” → the clearest explanation you will ever read.

Plus: African languages first — ask in isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Setswana, Kiswahili and more (22-language registry; generation activates at launch).
