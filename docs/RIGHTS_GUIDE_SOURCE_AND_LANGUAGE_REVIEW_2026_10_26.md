# LUQI-AI Rights Guide — public-source and language review contract

**Deployed source-linked public pilot: 26 October 2026 (Africa/Johannesburg). Independent professional legal approval remains outstanding.**
**Project:** ttmodupe-hash/luqi-ai / kind-spontaneity. This is NOT a live government feed.

## Purpose and safety

Offer free, accessible, source-linked general information, starting with South African asylum and refugee procedures. Every factual summary must link to its original sources and show the check date. This page is not legal advice or representation. LUQI can make mistakes; a qualified legal professional should be consulted for detention, deportation, rejections or deadlines.

The deployed public rights-guide route shows English source-linked text plus the status of Sepitori and other language-review candidates. No personally identifiable case details, biometrics, email, document uploads, account tokens, prompts, language preference persistence, government submission, paid AI call, checkout, browser microphone or automatic email is involved.

**Never invent deadlines, case eligibility, a personal asylum story, completed submissions, lawyer availability or government receipts.**

## What carries legal authority versus what is planned

- The **Refugees Act 130 of 1998 (as amended)** is legislation. The 7 July 2026 Constitutional Court judgment in *Scalabrini Centre* ([2026] ZACC 30) confirmed the invalidity of specified exclusion provisions. The Court's own media explanation expressly says that the media summary is not binding; cite the judgment itself for the legal ruling.
- The **11 October 2026 government briefing** described a planned intention-registration and appointment system expected by **10 November 2026**. This is a government announcement, **not a verified live service or a new enactment**. Before repeating procedural guidance, confirm any updated directive, actual operational availability, court order and legally relevant effective date.
- No unreviewed AI-generated answer is represented as authoritative legal advice. No personal eligibility, appeal deadline, detention outcome or current appointment availability is inferred from these general sources.

## Freshness and professional acceptance

- Source list checked 26 October 2026. Detailed summaries fail closed after 2 November 2026 (South African local time); source links and urgent referral remain visible.
- Government's planned 10 November procedure must be revalidated when actually implemented; an announcement is not deployment proof.
- Prefer primary legal sources for legal propositions, with UNHCR helping explain them. Review per-statement citations and record changes.
- Before public promotion as a legal information service, obtain qualified local legal review and privacy assessment. A source link is not professional signoff.
- Safeguard accuracy for time-critical decisions, including appeal and renewal notices. Never calculate a deadline from a generic summary.

## Sepitori and other language readiness

- Sepitori is distinct from standard Sepedi, Setswana and Sesotho. Do not silently replace one with another.
- UI exposes a language-review preference only. No unreviewed non-English legal translation is represented as available. English text is a source-linked draft, not an independently approved legal opinion.
- Use consent-based native-speaker and qualified legal-terminology review for each language, with versioned bilingual glossary, reversible official terms, synthetic scenarios, comprehension checks, and privacy-safe QA. Include regional variety and accessibility testing.
- Include Sepitori, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Setswana, Sepedi, Sesotho, Xitsonga, Tshivenda, siSwati, Shona, Kiswahili, Somali and other languages in prioritisation; none are production-certified here.
- Voice recognition, generation and translation require separate accuracy, user consent and retention acceptance. No model or microphone is invoked in this version.
