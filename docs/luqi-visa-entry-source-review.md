# Luqi Visa & Entry guide — original source navigator

Source review: 29 October 2026. Detailed nationality examples expire after 5 November 2026 without a human recheck. This is not a government account, eVisa application, immigration law opinion, booking engine or border clearance.

First five destination authorities:
- South Africa: Department of Home Affairs ETA, https://eta.dha.gov.za/ ; immigration categories, https://www.dha.gov.za/index.php/immigration-services and https://www.gov.za/services/temporary-residence/visa . As of 1 October 2026, government described an expanding ETA rollout for short stays and 53 nationalities, but not an exhaustive assured eligibility rule. Work is expressly excluded from ETA. Airports and passport details depend on official online conditions.
- Kenya: Directorate of Immigration Services https://etakenya.go.ke/how-to-apply and https://etakenya.go.ke/ . The original 2025 amended nationality exemptions include South Africa for a published stay cap of 90 days, but the category must be rechecked as a *dated published example*, not a live determination.
- Nigeria: Nigeria Immigration Service https://immigration.gov.ng/nigerian-visa/ and https://immigration.gov.ng/knowledge-base/ . Short visit eVisa classes differ from employment, residence and consular routes.
- Egypt: Government eVisa portal https://www.visa2egypt.gov.eg/ and FAQ https://www.visa2egypt.gov.eg/eVisa/FAQ . South Africa appears on the published eligible-to-apply eVisa list, not a visa-free or approval list. The official application page says start at least seven days before departure.
- Cameroon: Ministry of External Relations https://www.diplocam.cm/e-visa/ and official eVisaCam portal https://www.evisacam.cm/ . Verify current eligibility and local mission requirements.

The guide collects *only* a user-selected passport-issuing country (no number), destination, trip purpose, arrival method, intended date and stay length. There are no passports, ID numbers, biometrics, OTPs, payment details, case identifiers, government logins, location permission, uploads, automatic applications, fees, AI generation, or personal eligibility decisions. An on-device printable text checklist is not a government document or submission.

The independently scheduled **Luqi Visa and Immigration Watch** reviews official source notices daily; it alerts only on verified material changes. It cannot automatically edit this in-app static guide or make paid AI requests. Rule updates require review, tests, exact commit CI, Railway release and independent acceptance.
