# Africa and African Peoples in the Bible

Owner-approved follow-through to the supplied Forgotten Bible Stories review and the corrected historical/textual assessment, 1 November 2026. Luqi and KHORO remain one product. This is a learning module, not a claim to establish racial ancestry from scripture.

## Implementation
Adds `AfricanBiblicalStudy` within the existing `/comparative-evidence` page in both frontend trees. It is reached through the existing Faith, history & scientific evidence directory entry. Seven topics: Genesis geography; Canaan and the curse; Nimrod; Zerah/Taharqa; Ebed-Melech; Simon of Cyrene; Acts 8 and separately documented Aksumite Christianity. Each has four labelled views: text, evidence, interpretation and reflection. The module offers four evidence-reading practice questions with explicit citations and reset; it never grades a user's religion or stores belief profiles.

The seven existing comparisons and their guide/session-selection behaviour remain unchanged. Home.tsx, Moon tracker, Contacts rail, account handling, AI gateway, payments and budgets are untouched. The uploaded all-tools App.jsx is design context, not a substitute for canonical TSX source. No extra route or startup dependency is added.

## Editorial provenance and source boundaries
The supplied review determines the subject and Africa-centred educational purpose. The separately researched corrections were explicitly approved before implementation. The documentary UXjxKg96MCY has not been transcribed here, so neither its specific wording nor the accuracy of the review as a video summary is asserted. Reflection questions are new Luqi teaching activities, visibly labelled, not quotations. English biblical text links are labelled NABRE translations, not Hebrew/Greek originals. Independent academic and native-language review are not claimed.

Source edition 2026-11-01; re-review due 2027-02-01. Invalid/out-of-range dates withhold lesson summaries and quiz feedback while preserving the source library. This is a bounded source edition, not live scholarly browsing or an automated source-renewal promise.

Thirteen linked sources are defined with publisher, source kind, URL and precise scope. Checked through public pages/indexed source text during preparation:
- USCCB Genesis 9 and 10, Jeremiah 38 and 39, Mark 15, Acts 8, 2 Chronicles 14 and 2 Kings 19. Direct fetches of Jeremiah 38 and 2 Kings 19 were denied in the research environment; indexed excerpts from the same official publisher were inspected instead. This is not proof of universally accessible links.
- John Day, TheTorah.com (2022), Table of Nations geography; scholarly interpretation, not DNA evidence.
- Metropolitan Museum of Art, shabti 18.2.10, dated 690–664 BCE; material record.
- British Museum collection account of prism 1855,1003.1 via Google Arts & Culture, discussing the 701 BCE campaign; distinguish a royal narrative from neutral event coverage.
- UNESCO Cyrene site record for geography, not Simon's complexion.
- Ashmolean's archived Aksum coin exhibition for fourth-century Christian material evidence, not proof of a church founded by the Acts official.

No copyrighted chapter is reproduced; lesson prose is short paraphrase. No historical portrait, film, tracking embed or unverified video-derived statement is inserted. The implementation does not silently expand to Zipporah or named church fathers; those need their own sourced lesson review.
