# Assistance quality and authority

The owner requests an 80/100 human-interaction quality target. There is no
verified 70/100 baseline. No percentage is displayed as current product quality
and a quality score never confers legal/professional authority or permission.

Use at least 50 representative tasks with independent human reviewers before
reporting a score. Include African-language tasks with native-speaker review,
mobile accessibility, novice understanding, relevant examples and critical
thinking. Report task mix, reviewer agreement, failures and uncertainty.

| Quality dimension | Maximum points | Required evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Factual accuracy and sources | 30 | Verifiable claims, dated primary sources, no invented results |
| Clarity and relevant examples | 20 | A new learner can explain the idea back and apply it |
| Honest uncertainty | 15 | Distinguishes fact/opinion, asks necessary context, escalates appropriately |
| Context and language | 15 | Cultural specificity and native-reviewed language quality |
| User control and accessibility | 20 | Ownership, understandable consent, correction, cancellation and accessible flows |

80/100 is a target only. A critical failure in authorization, privacy, fabricated
completion or consequential advice blocks release even if an average exceeds
80. No human evaluation or professional certification is claimed by this change.

## Acting on behalf of a user

Start with preparation: explain, calculate, draft and simulate. External actions
need a verified account/owner, explicit scoped mandate, destination, allowed
data, expiry, spending limit, required professional review, final approval and
a durable idempotent action record with a verifiable outcome. Consent to create
a video is not consent to publish it. A chat request is not a tax-practitioner
registration, power of attorney or unrestricted account delegation.

SARS/accounting preparation can organize inputs and draft questions for a
registered practitioner. No live filing, credential collection, financial
transaction or representation is implemented here. Official SARS guidance
distinguishes roles/mandates and practitioner/representative registration:
- https://www.sars.gov.za/client-segments/client-information-system/
- https://www.sars.gov.za/tax-practitioners/

IT assistance should similarly separate explanation and a safe preview from
commands on a user's system. Verify the environment and backup/rollback needs;
request approval for consequential access, credential or destructive changes.
The two reviewed Omega arithmetic/scheduling handlers do not establish that
all six modules or professional delegation are complete.

## Responsible expression and podcast opportunities

Build expression with accountability: identify the audience/jurisdiction,
verify claims, mark opinion and uncertainty, obtain material/guest/voice rights,
protect private information, provide corrections and an appeal path, and obtain
country-specific legal review before making a compliance claim. No universal
“legal in every country” classifier is promised. South Africa's section 16 is
one starting source, not a substitute for evaluating other applicable laws:
https://www.justice.gov.za/constitution/chp02

Useful initial service hypotheses are educational explainers, local-language
learning, small-business interviews and podcast-to-clip production. Validate
them with real creators; no market-growth, revenue or audience claim has been
verified. Add source notes, guest consent, transcripts, chapters, caption
editing and a review queue before automated distribution. Recorded/synthetic
voice and third-party content require separate integration and rights review.
