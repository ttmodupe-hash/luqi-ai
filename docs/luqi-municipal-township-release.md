## Municipal directory, not account integration

The official government overview says **257 municipalities**: 8 metro, 44 district, 205 local: https://www.gov.za/about-government/government-system/local-government.

The national government contact directory currently exposes **255 municipality names**: https://www.gov.za/about-government/contact-directory/provincial-local-government. Luqi separately includes two additional verified municipality names absent from that directory snapshot:

- Collins Chabane Local Municipality, Limpopo: https://www.gov.za/links/local-government, also verified on the municipality's own website https://www.collinschabane.gov.za/.
- JB Marks Local Municipality, North West: https://www.gov.za/news/media-advisories/government-activities/deputy-minister-sello-seitlholo-conducts-oversight-0.

This makes **257 searchable names**, but not 257 validated municipal phone numbers, service endpoints, live outage statuses, exact address mappings or active trading portals. A user's manually chosen municipality may not have jurisdiction over a particular service or location; verify with the council and responsible utility.

Local guidance: https://www.gov.za/services/place-live/municipal-services and https://www.cogta.gov.za/index.php/2021/04/11/how-do-i-access-free-basic-municipal-services/. Free basic services require local indigent rules and means tests; no automated eligibility or fixed allocation claims.

## Township and rural small-business support

The 22 October 2026 DSBD **announcement** for its enhanced Township and Rural Entrepreneurship Programme (TREP) describes a relaunch planned for 27 October, financing up to R3 million, 5% interest, up to 60 months repayment and up to six months moratorium: https://www.dsbd.gov.za/minister-to-relaunch-enhanced-township-and-rural-entrepreneurship-programme/. An event announcement alone **does not verify a current open application window**, approve an applicant, or convert repayable finance into a grant. See older programme details at https://www.dsbd.gov.za/township-and-rural-entrepreneurship-programme-trep/ and verify new product-specific rules with DSBD/Sedfa.

The Spaza Shop Support Fund is separate: https://www.dsbd.gov.za/spaza-shop-support-fund-sssf/. Government cautions that municipal business registration is distinct from a trading licence and that compliance must be checked: https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/small-business-development-and-trade-industry-and-competition-update-spaza. Requirements may include zoning, health Certificate of Acceptability and the actual municipal trading permit. Do not treat an old registration deadline, financing maximum or press release as current acceptance.

## Explicit boundaries and review

No precise geolocation, government integration, access to a personal municipal account, claim, registration, lender decision, case filing, licence, tender submission or business finance guarantee. No identity, municipal account, SARS PIN, bank information, trade secret, password or OTP intake. No fees. An on-device downloaded checklist does **not** constitute a filing or evidence of acceptance.

Static programme information displays its own **review expiry**; publisher listing/reachability is never proof of current applications, live municipality services, or eligibility. A separate owner-only **Luqi Municipal and Township Economy Watch** performs daily scheduled original-notice checks, reporting only material validated changes. That agent is external to GitHub and is not an automatic government scraper or production content publishing pipeline. It must not edit live Luqi unreviewed, incur AI spend, or duplicate the existing opportunity and tender watchers.
