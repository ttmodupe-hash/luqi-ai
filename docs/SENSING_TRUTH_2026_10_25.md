# African Opportunity Radar — provenance and trade integrity (25 October 2026)

This candidate corrects the public pilot's fabricated market information. The older radar route generated arbitrary live viewer timestamps, asserted grant windows and budgets without a source, invented crop NDVI/soil moisture/yields, and applied a hard-coded 15%/2.5% duty calculation while asserting border clearance and PAPSS outcomes. Those outputs are not externally verified evidence.

The existing unreviewed sensing HTTP namespaces are now closed for every method with an explicit no-store 503 SENSING_SOURCE_UNVERIFIED response. This applies regardless of query parameters, forged authorisation, or future unreviewed subpaths. An existing legitimate budgeted text trial, account routes and the Business Desk are not reopened or altered.

The customer-facing /african-radar and /radar views are now a browser-local research and arithmetic page. It does not call providers, store inputs, promise open grants or publish false performance figures. The user provides the USD cargo value and BOTH assumed duty percentages. Exact integer-cent calculations show whether the second scenario would be higher, lower or equal. No implicit duty rates, parcel classifications, Rules of Origin or customs eligibility are supplied. This is not legal, tax or customs advice.

The page links to the African Union AfCFTA e-Tariff Book, South African dtic AfCFTA guidance, TIA open calls and Digital Earth Africa platforms as separately verifiable external sources. The destinations are not LUQI-connected integrations and the current status of individual grants, tariffs and crops must be independently verified.

Acceptance: unit tests for the pure arithmetic and invalid inputs, Hono tests ensuring legacy sensing is refused rather than invented, mobile/browser tests at 320px and WebKit, strict TypeScript compile, root/app UI parity and no automatic network calls.

Reopening any of the live endpoints requires a separately reviewed versioned API, source timestamps, verified original data and licences, budget/capacity/security controls and appropriate professional or operator oversight. No production deployment, merchant billing, provider key, subscription, database migration or live customer data change is approved by this PR. The preapproved USD100/month provider ceiling remains unchanged.
