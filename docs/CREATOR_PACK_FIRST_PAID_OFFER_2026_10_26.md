## Why this one

LUQI's browser-local Content Studio currently supports six editable 15-second scenes, a 90-second silent timeline, storyboard/script/subtitle exports, three aspect ratios and browser video capture where codec support permits. That is evidence of *technical feasibility*, not of a timed, repeatable human delivery process, live merchant approval or a customer accepting the result.

## Consumer terms that must be reviewed before selling

- South Africa's Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, **section 43**, requires supplier/transaction disclosures, an opportunity to review/correct/withdraw before ordering, delivery and refund information, and privacy/security details where applicable. Missing registered seller and support facts must not be invented.
- ECTA **section 44** generally provides a seven-day cooling-off period for qualifying online **services** after agreement. **Section 42(2)(d)** can exclude section 44 where performance began with the consumer's consent during that period. The exact applicability and consent evidence require a South African legal review. No blanket 'no refunds once work starts' rule is approved.
- The Consumer Protection Act **section 54** protects service quality and timely delivery and provides remediation or a reasonable refund where performance falls short. Statutory rights take precedence over the draft revision limit or any commercial cancellation wording.
- Before any early-start work, seek separately recorded, informed customer permission; do not assume a checkout click waives rights. Do not start/retain charge if order outcome is ambiguous. Define notice of delay, cure/refund response and non-delivery procedure.
- Customer-provided images and names need permission/licences. No unsolicited sensitive health, financial, ID, password or minor-related material should be accepted in an initial pilot. Obtain buyer approval of the final draft and deliver through a verified private channel.

Legal reference: https://www.saflii.org/za/legis/consol_act/ecata2002427/ (sections 42–44, 46), https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/32186_467.pdf (CPA section 54). These references do not constitute a legal sign-off.

1. **Merchant:** authorised owner confirms Paystack business is in verified **Live Mode**, seller/settlement currency, acceptable product category, public Live Key or fixed-amount merchant-owned Payment Page, and accessible dispute/refund controls. No secret key, OTP or bank record belongs in source, tickets or chat.
2. **Customer terms:** owner approves the exact ZA seller, final ZAR price, taxes, scope, working-day clock, revisions, cancellation/refund conditions, asset use/retention, verified private order-support contact and merchant display. The first public offer must match the actual merchant's seller and charge.
3. **Readiness:** operator demonstrates one complete, rights-cleared sample from customer brief through export and handover; verifies exact clip duration, all six scene changes, captions, soundless playback and accessible recipient file on a real device. Time the work and assess realistic order capacity.
4. **Receipt:** after a separately authorised live transaction, an operator verifies successful transaction status, **exact** ZAR amount, seller and unique reference in the merchant's Live dashboard. A browser redirect, Paystack callback, customer screenshot or emailed receipt alone cannot grant fulfilment or credits.
5. **Fulfilment:** operator privately acknowledges the order and delivery date, obtains approved input assets, records consent, prepares and reviews outputs, handles corrections, delivers/download-verifies files, and records resolution without publishing account content.
6. **Disputes:** operator is able to receive support, issue an appropriate refund/correction, resolve failed/duplicate attempts and keep an access-controlled record. No automated orders, subscriptions, wallet credits or credits-for-AI through the legacy disabled routes.
7. **Release:** only after human commercial acceptance, follow the exact manifest/digest + enabled guard in [hosted checkout](HOSTED_CHECKOUT_ACTIVATION_2026_10_26.md) or [public-key route](PAYSTACK_PUBLIC_KEY_CHECKOUT_2026_10_26.md).
