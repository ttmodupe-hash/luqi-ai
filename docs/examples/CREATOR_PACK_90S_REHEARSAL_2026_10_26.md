# LUQI Assisted Creator Pack — reproducible 90-second rehearsal

**Prepared:** 26 October 2026 SAST. **Status:** storyboard and SRT are source-only sample inputs, not a rendered customer video or evidence of human fulfilment. **Fictional demo — no real client, order or event.**

## Mock brief

Audience: someone who wants to tell a small-business, school or community story in clear, friendly language. Goal: demonstrate how one ordinary idea becomes a six-scene silent clip. Tone: practical, welcoming, accessible. The sample uses text and original LUQI-native shapes, without third-party photos, private user data, music or brand claims. Client selects **one** aspect ratio; start with landscape 16:9 for the rehearsal.

### Six-scene storyboard, exactly 90 seconds of planned timeline

| Scene | Timeline | Visual action to build with local studio tools | On-screen headline / caption |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 00:00–00:15 | A simple illustrated page, one highlighted question, slow movement | Every great idea begins with one question. |
| 2 | 00:15–00:30 | The question becomes a single opening scene with a title | Give your message a simple beginning. |
| 3 | 00:30–00:45 | Six connected storyboard frames appear in sequence | Build your story one clear scene at a time. |
| 4 | 00:45–01:00 | Show a high-contrast text card and adjustable caption position | Use readable captions so everyone can follow. |
| 5 | 01:00–01:15 | Show a spelling, format and timing review card | Check your details before you share. |
| 6 | 01:15–01:30 | Close on a neutral review/download card, not fake social-platform publishing | Your story, ready for your final review. |

Each scene should be configured to **15 seconds** in the existing Content Studio. Avoid copyrighted images, soundtracks, logos belonging to others, sensitive names and fabricated testimonials. Place emphasis on legible captions and clear transitions rather than unverified AI animation. Sample SRT: [creator-pack-story-demo-20261026.srt](examples/creator-pack-story-demo-20261026.srt). The 100-ms subtitle gap between cues is intentional and does not change scene duration.

## Draft plain-text script, for handover

1. Every great idea begins with one question.
2. Give your message a simple beginning.
3. Build your story one clear scene at a time.
4. Use readable captions so everyone can follow.
5. Check your details before you share.
6. Your story, ready for your final review.

This script is an accompanying text asset, **not synthesized or recorded narration**. The video must remain silent. Do not tell a buyer that the platform provided a voiceover or automatically posted the film.
