# LUQI founder revenue plan — hypotheses, NOT customer pricing

Updated: 25 October 2026, South Africa.

## Financial rule
The aim is customer-funded growth, not dependence on capital grants. Do not
buy new software or cloud services for a price experiment. Essentials and cash
runway come before additional providers. No estimated sales, leads or cash
receipts are counted as real income.

## Competitive context, as checked 25 October 2026
- Google's public ZA subscription page lists AI Plus at R104.99/month.
  https://gemini.google/za/subscriptions/?hl=en-GB
- The South African Apple Store catalog shows ChatGPT Go around R149.99/month
  and Plus R399.99/month. App prices may differ from web checkout.
  https://apppriceatlas.com/countries/za/
These are *not* like-for-like feature comparisons. Do not advertise LUQI as
having more advanced model quality or unlimited AI than these services.

## Two affordability tests
- R49/month: potential entry membership for basic productivity.
- R99/month: potential creator/small-business membership.
These amounts are **hypotheses** until a responsible operator verifies VAT and
all other tax treatment, actual AI consumption, expected fixed operating costs,
processor fees, user support and customer willingness to pay. Both are BELOW
Google's advertised R104.99 AI Plus price, but LUQI cannot honestly promise
equivalent or greater model/service entitlements.

The tools currently working for a pilot user are the local content studio,
saved notes, a physics lab, two Omega local calculations, bounded AI text
trial with separate consent and global cap, and this local business desk
(once deployed). All local tools remain free in the pilot. A paid membership
must offer *something newly implemented and clearly distinguishable from the
free pilot*, such as account-owned projects, approved exports, training/help
or independently verified support; those are not currently sold.

## Usage economics
- Approved provider project allocation: USD100 total per UTC month, Gemini
  USD60, OpenAI USD40. Existing code reserves $0.10 per attempted paid
  inference; fallbacks can reserve a second provider bucket. A reservation
  is NOT actual billed cost. A per-account daily attempt cap is not a monthly
  subscriber entitlement.
- Any proposed per-tier AI request bundle must sum to less than the shared
  remaining reservation budgets after active free-pilot usage, fallback
  allowances and operational safety buffers. Model request counts must be
  rejected by a durable authenticated entitlement ledger, not browser labels.
- Do NOT sell unlimited requests, paid plans, permanent priority, avatar
  creation, long AI video, live voice, autonomous agents or direct posting
  before those features and their operational costs pass real acceptance.
- Model net monthly contribution as actual subscription receipts minus
  taxes owed, processor fees, actual provider bills, hosting, support and
  refunds. Gross subscription value is not profit.

## Zero-monthly-fee processor candidates (not connected)
- Paystack South Africa pricing currently states 2.9% + R1 for local cards
  excluding VAT, with no upfront/monthly fee. Payment Pages support one-time
  or recurring checkout, but the merchant must first be verified and accepted.
  https://paystack.com/za/pricing
  https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2132546
- Yoco ZA advertises online payment links with no setup/monthly fee and
  transaction-based online pricing. Verify its exact fee, payout terms and
  merchant identity before choosing. A payment link alone is not a
  LUQI in-app subscription entitlement.
  https://www.yoco.com/za/online-payment/

Never paste merchant keys or private customer data into issue comments or CI.
Until a seller identity, supported processor, private support address,
refund/delivery terms, webhook-backed entitlement and actual acceptance exist,
the public Pricing page MUST say checkout is unavailable.

## Independent revenue steps, ordered
1. Invite a small, consenting pilot cohort to try the business desk and
   existing content studio; collect feedback without asking for payment.
2. Verify the creator can fulfil an optional, explicitly manual assisted
   storyboard or quotation-preparation service. Price only after establishing
   delivery capacity, support, corrections, rights, refunds and a verified
   merchant-owned link. No automatic fulfilment is claimed.
3. Observe repeat visits and explicit willingness to pay. Choose ONE paid
   use case with real, repeatable value before expanding premium scope.
4. Implement server-enforced entitlements and idempotent merchant callbacks
   only after safe recovery and security work. Reconcile actual spending
   before turning on any paid inference bundle.
5. Reinvest actual contribution from paying customers, not hypothetical
   signup numbers. Grants remain optional, not a dependency.
