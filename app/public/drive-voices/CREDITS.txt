Luqi-Drive / South African English narration · pack v1
These are synthetic introductions to Luqi's public province and corridor guides.
For each guide, cape/ uses the VCTK p314 Cape Town preset and joburg/ uses the
VCTK p347 Johannesburg preset. These names identify the corpus accent regions.
The featured places and transcripts are in src/features/drive/.

Produced offline in November 2026 with Piper (en_GB-vctk-medium, a multispeaker
model; speaker IDs 35 and 32 respectively) and converted to mono MP3. No model
weights, reference recordings or personal voice uploads are distributed here.
