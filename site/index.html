The Professor — a mentor that actually teaches

Type teach mathematics and a classroom opens. Online, Professor LUQI holds a real multi-turn conversation with full session context — it explains, asks one question at a time, and grades strictly. Offline, it never fakes a chat: you get an honest notice plus a real classroom with built-in 10-lesson syllabi and self-check quiz cards.

Conversational classroom

Rolling 40-message context keeps the professor on-topic. In-class commands: syllabus, quiz me, hint, next, progress, end class.

Syllabus & quizzes

syllabus <subject> renders a 10-lesson outline — LLM-generated online, 8 built-in curricula offline. Quizzes are graded and every score is recorded.

Persistent learner profile

progress shows lessons completed, average quiz score and weak points per subject — remembered across sessions in omega_memory.json.
