Grade 10 Physics Principles
1. Law of Conservation of Mechanical Energy: In an isolated system without air friction:
Etotal = Ek + Ep = constant

• At peak amplitude: Velocity = 0 → Ek = 0, Ep is at maximum (Ep = mgh = mgL(1 - cosθ)).
• At the equilibrium point (bottom): Height h = 0 → Ep = 0, all energy has transformed into maximum kinetic energy (Ek = ½mv²).

2. Period of a Simple Pendulum:
T = 2π √(L / g)
Key insight for Grade 10 exams: The period depends solely on length L and gravity g. It is independent of mass m and amplitude (for small angles θ < 15°).
