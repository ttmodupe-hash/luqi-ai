# Ubuntu — the design brief of LUQI AI

> *Umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu* — a person is a person through other people.

LUQI AI is built on ubuntu, the African humanist philosophy of the Nguni peoples:
our humanity is realised through each other. This file is the binding record of
what that means for how this product is designed, built and run. It is not
marketing copy — it is the same kind of law as the anti-hallucination rules in
the engine.

## The four commitments

1. **We protect each other.**
   Scam Shield exists because one person's verified red flag is the whole
   community's armour. Protection features are never paywalled away from the
   people most targeted by fraud.

2. **Knowledge is shared, not sold back.**
   Every answer shows its sources. Guides are written in plain language, in the
   languages people actually speak. The African History Archive is an
   inheritance returned to the people it describes — free to read, free to
   check.

3. **Dignity by default.**
   No fake testimonials. No invented statistics. No shame-based design, no dark
   patterns. When the platform does not know, it says "unverified" — an honest
   answer is worth more than a confident guess.

4. **Community trust is infrastructure.**
   Stokvels moved billions of rand on trust alone, generations before fintech
   existed. LUQI AI treats community trust the same way: earned slowly, spent
   never. Every feature is judged by whether it strengthens or erodes it.

## What this means in practice

- The receipts on our landing page are always real numbers that can be traced
  to the public engine repository.
- Services guides (SASSA, SARS, UIF, NSFAS) point only to official channels.
- Money moved through the platform follows one auditable path with human gates.
- Pricing, when it arrives, keeps a free tier that never locks out the people
  the mission is for.

## Where it lives in the product

- Landing page — the "Built on ubuntu" section.
- African History Archive — `ubuntu-philosophy`, `ubuntu-makwanyane-1995`

- Engine — the fail-closed, sources-or-silence design of every zero-cost
  router.
